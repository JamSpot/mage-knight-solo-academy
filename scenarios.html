Scenarios & expansions

The long-term aim is to cover every scenario and relevant expansion in your collection, with clear component lists, setup checks, objectives, timing and strategic notes.

Edition scope: this guide covers the Mage Knight Board Game and its three board-game expansions: The Lost Legion, Krang, and Shades of Tezla. WizKids confirms these are included in Ultimate Edition. Products from the separate Mage Knight miniatures game are outside this scope.

Scenario page template

Each completed scenario guide will include:

  • Required game and expansion components.
  • Player count and scenario goal.
  • Setup steps and map construction.
  • Round limit, special timing and end conditions.
  • Rules exceptions and common mistakes.
  • Beginner plan, advanced strategy and example decisions.
  • Official source references and any unresolved questions.
