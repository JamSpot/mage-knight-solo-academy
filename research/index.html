RESEARCH NOTEBOOK

Sources, scope & progress

A transparent record of where rules come from, what remains to be checked, and what has actually been completed.

Source register

  1. Official Mage Knight rule books — primary source for rules PDFs and edition-specific reference.
  2. WizKids: Mage Knight Ultimate Edition — product scope and official product information.
  3. Physical rulebooks and scenario sheets from the user's collection — to be checked when available, especially for edition-specific differences.
  4. Community strategy and rules discussions — secondary sources; useful for tactics and locating questions, but not treated as authoritative when they conflict with official rules.

Research standards

  • Label official rules, strategy advice and house rules separately.
  • Record edition and expansion assumptions.
  • Prefer primary sources and link to the source material.
  • Do not mark a scenario or rules entry complete until checked.
  • Use original explanatory text; do not reproduce entire copyrighted rulebooks.

Contribute the missing details

As research continues, the most helpful evidence is the exact edition and rulebook text for any disputed timing question, plus scenario sheets for expansions. The site will use those to build reliable, reproducible examples.
