Combat: the planned step-by-step reference

  1. Identify the enemy group and read all abilities and resistances.
  2. Determine which attack types are legal at the current stage.
  3. Calculate ranged and siege attacks when permitted.
  4. Resolve blocking and any applicable enemy abilities in the correct order.
  5. Resolve the attack phase and determine damage that must be absorbed.
  6. Assign damage to units or the hero as allowed by the rules, then resolve wounds and casualties.
  7. Check whether the encounter is defeated and resolve its rewards.

This is a topic outline, not a substitute for the exact timing and exception rules. A detailed combat example will be added after verification.
