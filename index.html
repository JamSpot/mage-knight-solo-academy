OUR METHOD

Rules first. Strategy second.

Each guide will separate official rules from strategy advice and community variants. Examples should be playable at the table, not merely theoretical.

Expansion material will be identified by product and scenario so you can quickly tell which components and rules are needed.

Academy principles

  • Navigation on every page
  • Readable light theme
  • Practical worked examples
  • Sources and confidence noted
  • Solo and two-player differences called out
  • Publish useful material in stages
