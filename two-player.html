TWO-PLAYER ACADEMY

Plan together—or outplay each other

Understand how player interaction, turn order, shared objectives and scenario rules change the decisions you make.

Guide in development

1. Decide what kind of game you are playing

Mage Knight supports different scenario structures. Before setup, confirm whether the selected scenario is competitive or cooperative, whether players share a victory condition, and how its time limit and end conditions work. Never assume that a cooperative strategy applies to a competitive scenario.

2. Coordination in cooperative play

  • Agree on broad roles: exploration, recruitment, experience-building or preparing a major assault.
  • Share information about threats and routes, while respecting the scenario's restrictions on communication or resources.
  • Avoid both players spending turns on the same low-value objective when the map offers better complementary opportunities.
  • Coordinate arrival timing for dangerous sites and city assaults; arriving separately can change the risk and opportunity.
  • Check the actual scenario rules for combat participation, shared rewards and any limits on trading or helping.

3. Competitive play

  • Read the victory conditions first; a high fame total is not necessarily enough if the scenario has another objective.
  • Track the other player's position and likely tempo without sacrificing every efficient move to interference.
  • Evaluate whether a contested location is worth the detour and opportunity cost.
  • Understand exactly how the scenario ends and how ties are resolved before the final round.

4. Two-player discussion before the first turn

  1. Which scenario and edition/expansion rules are being used?
  2. What ends the game, and how is the winner determined?
  3. What information can be shared and what actions can help or hinder the other player?
  4. Which map objectives are worth prioritising?
  5. How will you keep rounds, dummy-player progress (if any) and scenario effects in sync?

5. Planned worked examples

This page will grow to include a sample opening for competitive play, a cooperative city assault, a timing comparison between players, and expansion-specific notes. Each example will state the scenario assumptions so it can be reproduced at the table.

Important: the two-player rules are scenario-dependent. Confirm the chosen scenario's exact procedures before applying any generic advice above.
